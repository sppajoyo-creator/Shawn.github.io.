My Story

My name is Shawnpaulpajoyo, and I come from Binagasbasan Garchitorena Philippines. I grew up in a simple and supportive family that taught me to work hard and never give up, even when things get difficult. Growing up in Bacoor helped me become independent and curious about how things work, especially technology. From a young age, I was always interested in computers, phones, and how websites and programs are made.Currently, I am studying Information System and learning programming languages like C++ and web development with HTML. I enjoy creating simple programs like a grade computation system and a cashier receipt system, because they help me understand logic, problem-solving, and how real systems work. I also enjoy exploring new tools, watching tutorials, and practicing coding until I fully understand it. For me, every error is not a failure, but a chance to learn and improve.What drives me is my dream to become a successful IS professional and to help my family in the future. I want to use my skills to build useful websites and programs that can help people in their daily lives. I know I still have a lot to learn, but I am motivated, patient, and willing to keep practicing. My goal is to continue growing as a programmer and to one day create projects that I can be truly proud of.
